The journey / seven stamps

First call to first day abroad.

One team stays with the student through all seven steps. Each step is stamped only when the paperwork for it is actually done.

Checked at every step
01STEP

Free counselling

It starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. We study the student profile first and only take a case forward when the destination is a genuine fit. Counselling costs nothing.

02STEP

Test preparation

Guidance and preparation support for IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, GRE and GMAT, matched to what the shortlisted universities actually ask for.

03STEP

University shortlisting and applications

A shortlist drawn from 1,500 plus partner universities across 41 plus countries, then complete application handling, deadlines included.

04STEP

SOP, LOR and essay guidance

Statements of purpose, letters of recommendation and application essays shaped around the student record, in the student own voice.

05STEP

Scholarship strategy

Every eligible scholarship is mapped before applications go out. Our students have secured $100M plus in scholarships across destinations.

06STEP

Visa documentation and interview preparation

File preparation document by document, plus mock interviews before the real one. The result is a 98% visa success rate.

07STEP

Pre-departure briefing and post-arrival support

What to pack, what to expect, whom to call. The support continues after landing, not just until the flight leaves.
