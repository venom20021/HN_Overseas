Study in Canada

A clear line from classroom to career to PR.

Canada's Post-Graduation Work Permit lets eligible graduates work for up to three years after their studies — one of the most straightforward study-to-settle routes anywhere.

The Route

How the Canada journey runs.

Undergraduate route

  1. 01

    Profile first

    Class 12 marks and stream checked against college/university entry and PGWP-eligible programmes only.

  2. 02

    Shortlist

    Programmes chosen for employability and location, not just admission ease.

  3. 03

    Applications

    Direct applications with transcripts and language scores, tracked to offer.

  4. 04

    Study permit

    A complete permit file — finances, study plan and ties — prepared and cross-checked.

  5. 05

    Fly

    Briefing on SIN, work rules during study and how PGWP eligibility is protected.

Canada intakes (UG): Fall (Sep start) · Winter (Jan start) · Spring (May start)

Postgraduate route

  1. 01

    Profile first

    Degree and any work experience assessed; PGWP-eligible programmes shortlisted around your field.

  2. 02

    Applications

    Direct applications with a SOP that explains your study plan clearly — it matters for the permit too.

  3. 03

    Offer & fees

    Offer secured and first-term fee payment sequenced correctly for the permit file.

  4. 04

    Study permit

    Finances, GIC-equivalent proof where applicable, and a study plan that answers the officer's questions before they are asked.

  5. 05

    Fly

    Briefing on co-op terms, PGWP timing and the PR routes your work experience will open.

Canada intakes (PG): Fall (Sep start) · Winter (Jan start) · Spring (May start)
