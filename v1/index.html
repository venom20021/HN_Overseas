Lucknow → Milan

Public universities. Near-zero tuition. A scholarship that covers your living.

Italy's public universities charge an enrolment fee, not tuition — and eligible students receive a need-based regional scholarship with a yearly stipend, hostel and meals covered. It is the most affordable quality route in Europe, and it is the desk HN Overseas is known for.

Tuition
