Study in Italy

Public universities. Near-zero tuition. A scholarship that covers your living.

Italy's public universities charge an enrolment fee, not tuition — and eligible students receive a need-based regional scholarship with a yearly stipend, hostel and meals covered. It is the most affordable quality route in Europe, and it is the desk HN Overseas is known for.

The Route

How the Italy journey runs.

Undergraduate route

  1. 01

    Profile first

    Your Class 12 marks, subject fit and family budget are analysed before anything is filed — Italy rewards the right profile, not the fastest one.

  2. 02

    MOI & documents

    Most universities accept a Medium of Instruction certificate from your school, so IELTS is often not needed. Transcripts are attested and translated where required.

  3. 03

    University applications

    Applications to public universities for the single September intake, with the scholarship file prepared alongside — the two run together.

  4. 04

    Offer & pre-enrolment

    On your offer, we complete pre-enrolment and the Declaration of Value so your Indian marks are recognised in Italy.

  5. 05

    Visa filing

    Study visa file with finances, accommodation proof and insurance, checked document by document before submission.

  6. 06

    Fly

    Pre-departure briefing: residence permit (permesso), tax code, and how the scholarship instalments actually reach you.

Italy intakes (UG): September (Sep start)

Postgraduate route

  1. 01

    Profile first

    Your bachelor's marks and subject match are assessed against Italian public-university requirements before a shortlist is made.

  2. 02

    MOI & documents

    Most universities accept a Medium of Instruction certificate from your university; IELTS only where a specific course asks for it.

  3. 03

    University applications

    Master's applications for the September intake, with the need-based scholarship application prepared in parallel.

  4. 04

    Offer & pre-enrolment

    Pre-enrolment and Declaration of Value completed on your offer letter, so your degree is formally recognised.

  5. 05

    Visa filing

    A complete, cross-checked study-visa file — finances, accommodation, insurance — submitted once, correctly.

  6. 06

    Fly

    Briefing on the residence permit, part-time work rules (20 hrs/week) and the Erasmus semester option inside Europe.

Italy intakes (PG): September (Sep start)
